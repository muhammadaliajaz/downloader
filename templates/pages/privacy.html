Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Stellar Studio (accessible from aryplusdownloader.tech), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is received and collected by Stellar Studio and how it is used.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional platforms, Stellar Studio does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identity information (such as credit card details, full names, or home addresses) to utilize our video link downloader.

2. Temporary File Storage & Auto-Purge Policy

When a video extraction or MP3 conversion is triggered, the media is processed temporarily on high-performance servers. We implement an automated background cleanup scheduler that permanently deletes all temporary video files, audio tracks, and cached assets within 180 seconds after download completion.

3. Log Files & Rate Limiting

To prevent Denial-of-Service (DoS) abuse and protect service uptime, our system tracks anonymous client IP addresses strictly in memory for temporary sliding-window rate limiting (10 requests per minute). These records are automatically discarded and are never shared with advertisers.

4. Third-Party Links

Our blog articles may contain external hyperlinks to official channels (such as YouTube, ARY Digital Asia, TikTok, and technical documentation). We encourage our users to be aware when they leave our site and to read the privacy statements of each external website.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page.
